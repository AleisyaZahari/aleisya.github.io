Portfolio Details

This project classifies diseases and pests on cotton leaves using a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) built from scratch, aiming to help farmers detect crop issues early and reduce misidentification-driven losses.

Project information

  • Category: Machine Learning
  • Role: Individual (Undergraduate Thesis)
  • Project date: Jan 2024 – Jun 2024
  • Tools: Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Google Colab
  • Dataset: Cotton Leaf Diseases & Pests (self-collected image dataset, 6 classes)
  • Github: GitHub Repository
  • Website project: Open in Google Colab

🌿 Cotton Leaf Disease & Pest Classification (CNN) – Undergraduate Thesis

🎯 Goals:
Automate the identification of cotton leaf diseases and pests to replace slow, error-prone manual inspection, enabling faster and more accurate treatment decisions.

📈 Data Description:
The dataset covers 6 categories: three diseases (Bacterial Blight, Powdery Mildew, Target Spot), two pests (Aphids, Army Worm), and Healthy Leaves. Split into training (75%), validation (15%), and testing (10%) sets.

📈 Results:
Overall accuracy 91% (330 of 361 test images correctly classified) — Healthy Leaves 60/60 (100%), Aphids 58/65 correct, Army Worm 56/60 correct, Bacterial Blight 53/60 correct, Target Spot 52/61 correct.
